# Boba Fett

Boba Fett was a bounty hunter known for selective contracts, operational secrecy, and mobility across fragmented jurisdictions. He used Mandalorian armor and maintained a reputation for reliability under high-risk assignments.

His activity intersected with both imperial and criminal networks, including jobs linked to Han Solo and the Hutt Cartel. Boba Fett represents the non-state power layer in the galactic conflict economy.
