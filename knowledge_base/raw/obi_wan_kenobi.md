# Obi-Wan Kenobi

Obi-Wan Kenobi served as a Jedi Master during the late Republic and through the Clone Wars. He mentored Anakin Skywalker and later Luke Skywalker, emphasizing discipline and interpretation of the Force beyond aggression.

After the rise of the Empire and Order 66, Obi-Wan lived in isolation near Tatooine while monitoring long-term resistance prospects. His archived teachings influenced post-war reconstruction of Jedi traditions.
