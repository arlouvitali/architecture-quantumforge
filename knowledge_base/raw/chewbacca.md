# Chewbacca

Chewbacca, a Wookiee from Kashyyyk, served as co-pilot of the Millennium Falcon and a long-term ally of Han Solo. He combined technical skill with frontline resilience in both sabotage and extraction missions.

Although he rarely acted as a formal spokesperson, Chewbacca played a stabilizing role across the Rebel Alliance network and remained trusted by Leia Organa and Luke Skywalker.
