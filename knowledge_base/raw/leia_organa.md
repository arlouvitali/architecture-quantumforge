# Leia Organa

Leia Organa was a political leader tied to Alderaan and an operational organizer in the Rebel Alliance. She coordinated logistics, diplomacy, and intelligence across multiple cells resisting the Galactic Empire.

While many people knew her as a senator, Leia also demonstrated deep sensitivity to the Force, a trait shared within the Skywalker family. Her leadership style balanced military urgency with institution building after the war.
