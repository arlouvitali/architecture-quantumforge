# Anakin Skywalker

Anakin Skywalker emerged as a highly gifted Force user during the Republic era and fought extensively in the Clone Wars. His transition from Jedi Knight to Darth Vader became a defining institutional failure of the Jedi Order.

His trajectory reflected concentrated war pressure, political manipulation by Palpatine, and unresolved personal attachments. The case is often studied as a warning about command structures during prolonged conflict.
