# Luke Skywalker

Luke Skywalker grew up on Tatooine and originally planned to leave his moisture farm life for pilot training. During the Galactic Civil War, he joined the Rebel Alliance and became one of the most visible Jedi figures after the fall of the old Jedi Order.

He trained under Obi-Wan Kenobi and later Yoda to understand the Force, control a lightsaber, and face Darth Vader. Luke was known for preferring redemption over revenge, and his choices influenced the final collapse of the Empire.
