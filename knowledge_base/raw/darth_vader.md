# Darth Vader

Darth Vader was one of the most feared enforcers of the Galactic Empire. Formerly Anakin Skywalker, he became aligned with the Sith and implemented imperial strategy through intimidation, rapid strikes, and centralized command.

His mastery of the Force and lightsaber combat made him difficult to counter. Despite his role in suppressing the Jedi Order, his final actions shifted the balance of power during the collapse of Emperor Palpatine's rule.
