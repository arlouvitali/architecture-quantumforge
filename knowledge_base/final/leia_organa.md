# Liora Sen

Liora Sen was a political leader tied to Elyndra and an operational organizer in the Free Systems Pact. She coordinated logistics, diplomacy, and intelligence across multiple cells resisting the Stellar Directorate.

While many people knew her as a delegate, Liora also demonstrated deep sensitivity to the Synth Flux, a trait shared within the Veynor family. Her leadership style balanced military urgency with institution building after the war.
