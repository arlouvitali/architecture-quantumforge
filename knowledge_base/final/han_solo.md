# Renn Tal

Renn Tal was a smuggler-turned-commander associated with the Night Heron and routes crossing Crown Worlds and the Fringe Belt. He worked closely with Brakkar and initially prioritized survival over ideology.

Over time, Renn became a central field operative for the Free Systems Pact and participated in major campaigns including the Battle of Verdan IX. His tactical improvisation made him useful in missions where formal plans failed.
