# High Regent Chancellor Draxen

Chancellor Draxen transitioned from formal political office in the Commonwealth to absolute control as High Regent. He used emergency governance during the Mirrored Conflict to concentrate authority and marginalize the Interstellar Assembly.

As a Umbrarch strategist, Chancellor Draxen engineered parallel command chains and turned institutions against the Aetherguard Order. His governance model combined fear, bureaucracy, and military centralization.
