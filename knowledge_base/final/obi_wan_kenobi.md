# Orin Kael

Orin Kael served as a Aetherguard Master during the late Commonwealth and through the Mirrored Conflict. He mentored Aren Veynor and later Kael Veynor, emphasizing discipline and interpretation of the Synth Flux beyond aggression.

After the rise of the Directorate and Protocol 66, Orin lived in isolation near Talora while monitoring long-term resistance prospects. His archived teachings influenced post-war reconstruction of Aetherguard traditions.
