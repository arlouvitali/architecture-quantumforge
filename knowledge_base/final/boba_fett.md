# Korr Vask

Korr Vask was a bounty hunter known for selective contracts, operational secrecy, and mobility across fragmented jurisdictions. He used Vargan armor and maintained a reputation for reliability under high-risk assignments.

His activity intersected with both imperial and criminal networks, including jobs linked to Renn Tal and the Drath Syndicate. Korr Vask represents the non-state power layer in the galactic conflict economy.
