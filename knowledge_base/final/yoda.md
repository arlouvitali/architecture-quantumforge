# Maev Torin

Maev Torin was a senior Aetherguard Master and one of the final authorities on pre-Directorate Synth Flux doctrine. He spent centuries training Aetherguard and defining standards for how knowledge, restraint, and leadership should interact.

After Protocol 66, Maev Torin relocated to Mirefall and continued limited mentorship. His direct training of Kael Veynor was short in duration but strategic in impact for the Free Systems Pact.
