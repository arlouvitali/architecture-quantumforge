# Xarn Velgor

Xarn Velgor was one of the most feared enforcers of the Stellar Directorate. Formerly Aren Veynor, he became aligned with the Umbrarch and implemented imperial strategy through intimidation, rapid strikes, and centralized command.

His mastery of the Synth Flux and phase blade combat made him difficult to counter. Despite his role in suppressing the Aetherguard Order, his final actions shifted the balance of power during the collapse of High Regent Chancellor Draxen's rule.
