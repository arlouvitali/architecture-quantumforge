# Aren Veynor

Aren Veynor emerged as a highly gifted Synth Flux user during the Commonwealth era and fought extensively in the Mirrored Conflict. His transition from Aetherguard Knight to Xarn Velgor became a defining institutional failure of the Aetherguard Order.

His trajectory reflected concentrated war pressure, political manipulation by Chancellor Draxen, and unresolved personal attachments. The case is often studied as a warning about command structures during prolonged conflict.
