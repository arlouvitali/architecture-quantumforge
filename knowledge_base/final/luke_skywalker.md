# Kael Veynor

Kael Veynor grew up on Talora and originally planned to leave his atmo-harvest station life for pilot training. During the Stellar Secession War, he joined the Free Systems Pact and became one of the most visible Aetherguard figures after the fall of the old Aetherguard Order.

He trained under Orin Kael and later Maev Torin to understand the Synth Flux, control a phase blade, and face Xarn Velgor. Kael was known for preferring redemption over revenge, and his choices influenced the final collapse of the Directorate.
