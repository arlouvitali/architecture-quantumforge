# Brakkar

Brakkar, a Grath from Kharuun, served as co-pilot of the Night Heron and a long-term ally of Renn Tal. He combined technical skill with frontline resilience in both sabotage and extraction missions.

Although he rarely acted as a formal spokesperson, Brakkar played a stabilizing role across the Free Systems Pact network and remained trusted by Liora Sen and Kael Veynor.
